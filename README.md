## Avoiding Common TopCashback Mistakes

Understanding how cashback works isn't enough—you also need to know what can go wrong and how to prevent problems that could cost you earnings.

**Using External Coupons**: The biggest mistake is using a promo code you found independently rather than one linked directly from TopCashback. Many retailers void cashback rewards if you use unauthorized coupon codes. Always use coupons found through the TopCashback platform to ensure your purchase remains eligible.

**Not Reading Retailer-Specific Terms**: Different retailers have different restrictions. Some exclude certain product categories from cashback eligibility. Others require you to meet minimum purchase amounts. Always check the specific terms for each retailer before purchasing.

**Using Cookies Tracking**: Disable browser extensions that interfere with cookie tracking (the technology that tracks you came through TopCashback), like some ad blockers or privacy tools. TopCashback needs cookies enabled to track your purchase.

**Shopping Through Apps**: Most cashback tracking only works on the retailer's website. Shopping through the native app instead of their website often means your cashback doesn't register. Always shop through the website and verify the TopCashback extension is active.

**Gift Card Purchases on Bonus Offers**: Many retailers explicitly exclude gift card purchases from bonus rate offers (though base cashback may still apply). Check the details before buying gift cards expecting to earn the promotional rate.

**Not Waiting for Confirmation**: Your cashback must reach "Confirmed" status before it's eligible for payout. If you request payment before it's confirmed and something goes wrong with the transaction, you might not get your money. Wait for confirmation status.

**Assuming Your Cashback Will Appear Instantly**: Patience is key. The three-stage process (Pending → Confirmed → Payable) can take up to 90 days. Don't assume something failed if it hasn't appeared immediately.
